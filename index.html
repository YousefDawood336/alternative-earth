Species at risk

Who we could lose, and when.

Animals whose survival is tied to ice, reefs, forests, coasts or cool mountains. For each period on the timeline, the species most likely to be lost and the chance it goes extinct by the end of that period.

Extinction probability by period

How this is estimated: each species starts from a yearly risk set by its IUCN Red List category (Least Concern → Critically Endangered). That risk is multiplied by how exposed the species is to the climate factors in this site's model: sea-ice loss, coral loss, sea level, heat, fire, drought, deforestation and ocean acidification. These follow the scenario and the sliders in the 3D View. This is an illustrative risk index for comparing species and scenarios, not an official IUCN projection. Ask ÉcoVie

Ask ÉcoVie.

ÉcoVie (French for “eco-life”) answers only from the NASA climate data and official references on this site: GISTEMP temperatures, GISS CO₂, NASA technical reports, the NCA5 / IPCC scenarios, and the Space Apps resources. It does not search the internet and politely declines questions outside climate and this website.

The Alternative Earth

Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge to show how human choices shape Earth's next 300 years. Data: NASA GIBS · GISTEMP · SRTM · EONET · EPIC · NTRS · Image & Video Library, and the Fifth National Climate Assessment (NCA5). Projections follow the IPCC AR6 / NCA5 scenario pathways. A simplified emulator for exploration, not a forecast. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
